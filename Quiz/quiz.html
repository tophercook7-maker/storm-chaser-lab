Why the words matter

A trained spotter reports what they see in plain, agreed-on terms so a forecaster 100 miles away pictures it exactly. "Wall cloud, rotating, on the southwest flank" means one specific thing. Fuzzy words waste the few minutes a warning buys. Precision is the job.
