The report format

The National Weather Service wants four things: what you saw, where, when, and who you are. Be specific and don't guess. "Quarter-size hail 3 miles south of town at 4:15" beats "big hail" every time. If you aren't sure what you saw, say you aren't sure — that's useful too, and it protects your credibility.

Time, temperature and dewpoint fill in automatically from the nearest weather station.
