What this certificate is (and isn't)

A certificate here means one thing: you finished this lab's training for that rank. That's a personal milestone, and it's worth being proud of.

It is not a government license. The one official, government-recognized credential in this whole lab is SKYWARN spotter training, run by the National Weather Service. These certificates are the map that walks you toward it — Rookie and Spotter get you ready to take the real class.
